Safe to drive. Below — how much time you have and what happens if you keep going.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: Enough to reach the yard

This is not the fault, it is the reaction to it: the ECU is cutting power to protect the engine or the aftertreatment.

The limit will keep tightening until the cause is fixed. The code to chase is the one that triggered this, not this one.

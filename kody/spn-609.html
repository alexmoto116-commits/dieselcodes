SPN 609 — Controller #2

Drive with cautionEnough to reach the yard

Updated: 2026-11-14

J1939 code SPN 609. Below — how the fault is worded at the factory by KAMAZ-5490, Knorr-Bremse EBS, MAN, Mercedes-Benz and Renault Trucks, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means in the standard.

How to check it yourself

Controller No. 2 — an internal fault in the second unit in the system (on different makes this is a different unit: a second processor, a body unit, a gearbox unit). Check it the same way as controller No. 1 (SPN 629).

What it should read

Supply at the unit connector under loadas at the battery, sag not more than 0.5 V

Check order

  1. Find out from the factory description which unit is this make's "controller No. 2".
  2. Its power, grounds and connectors.
  3. The link between the units — if the code comes with FMI 9 or 19, it is lost messages, not a breakdown.
  4. Update the software, then replace.

Common causes — from most to least frequent

  1. Power or ground of the unit.
  2. Link between the units.
  3. Faulty unit.

Can I keep driving

Drive with caution. The ABS on this wheel or axle may be off, but the service brakes work. On a slippery road brake gently. Get it repaired soon.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: Enough to reach the yard

The ABS on this wheel or axle may be out of action, while the service brakes still work. On a dry road you may notice no difference.

On a slippery road brake gently and keep a longer following distance. Do not put the repair off: if the red brake lamp comes on or the pedal feels soft, stop.

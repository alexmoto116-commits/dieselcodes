SPN 94 — Fuel delivery pressure

Safe to driveTo a stop or a shop

Updated: 2026-11-10

J1939 code SPN 94. Below — how the fault is worded at the factory by Foton, KAMAZ-5490 and Renault Trucks, and what the second half of the code (FMI) means in the standard.

How to check it yourself

Fuel pressure in the supply system: a pressure sensor (its location depends on the make). The unit uses it to see whether there is enough pressure for injection. Low pressure can cause loss of power and the engine stopping.

What it should read

Fuel pressure at idle and under loadper the manufacturer's manual
Sensor signal with the engine offneither zero nor at plus — per diagnostics

Check order

  1. Pressure below normal: check the filters, the transfer pump and the air on the suction side (clear tube, bubbles); in winter, whether the fuel has waxed.
  2. FMI 3 or 4 (electrical): unplug the sensor connector and measure to plus and to ground with the ignition on.
  3. Pressure above normal: check the sensor and the pressure regulator; if it repeats — per the injection system troubleshooting guide.

Common causes — from most to least frequent

  1. Clogged fuel filters.
  2. In winter — wax in summer-grade fuel and a filter clogged by it.
  3. Air leak on the suction side.
  4. Transfer pump or pressure regulator.
  5. Pressure sensor or its connector.

Can I keep driving

You can drive to the nearest stop or a shop if power is normal. If power drops, the engine misfires or stalls — stop.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: To a stop or a shop

The fuel pressure is out of range, and the engine may not get enough fuel. It can show up as a loss of power and hesitation uphill and under load, and in a severe case the engine stopping.

If power is normal you can make it to the nearest stop or a shop; if power drops, the engine jerks or stops, stop. First check the fuel filters and air leaks on the suction side, and in winter fuel waxing.

This is an electrical fault: an open, a short or a bad contact in the sensor circuit. The value it measures is most likely fine. You can drive. But until the circuit is repaired this system runs blind and cannot warn you about a real failure.

What to check on the spot

Check the fuel filter and the water trap, drain the water. In winter add fuel waxing to the suspects: summer-grade diesel in the cold and a filter clogged by it. If the FMI is 3 or 4 it is electrical: inspect the sensor connector and harness.

Which codes follow

If the value really did go out of range, rather than a sensor circuit being broken, these normally light up after it:

  • SPN 652Engine Injector, Cylinder #02
  • SPN 653Engine Injector, Cylinder #03
  • SPN 655Engine Injector, Cylinder #05
  • SPN 1569Torque limited (engine protection)

They are not fixed separately — they go out together with this one. The list is deliberately short: only the links the reference is sure about.

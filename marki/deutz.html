Deutz codes: what to know

Deutz has a dealer table: 1057 codes. The most common areas are the exhaust and AdBlue system (SCR), fuel and intake air. Codes such as 100.0 and 100.1 look like SPN and FMI, but the Deutz table has its own scheme, so take the meaning from the description. For example, 100.0 is critical overpressure of the engine oil.

About these Deutz codes

Deutz engines power construction machines, farm tractors, forklifts, generator sets and some buses far more often than long-haul tractors, so many of these codes come from the machine's own display rather than a truck dashboard. The engine computer (EMR, Electronic Motor Regelung) stores faults in two forms: J1939 style SPN.FMI separated by a dot, and Deutz-specific numbers above 516000. The manufacturer reads them with its SerDia diagnostic software.

The difference matters when reading: 100.0 is the standard SPN 100 (engine oil pressure) with FMI 0, while 516408.31 is an internal Deutz number that does not exist in the J1939 tables.

Reading the FMI part

  • FMI 0, 15, 16 - above normal (0 is the most severe): overheating or excess pressure.
  • FMI 1, 17, 18 - below normal (1 is the most severe).
  • FMI 3 and 4 - circuit voltage high or low, usually an open circuit or a short.
  • FMI 5 and 6 - current below normal (open) and current above normal (short to ground).
  • FMI 2 - erratic or implausible data; AdBlue sensor entries show it often.
  • FMI 11 and 31 - cause not known or condition exists: use the engine manual.

FMI meanings follow SAE J1939-73; the tables above give the factory wording of each Deutz entry.

Common causes, most frequent first

  • Connectors and harness: moisture and vibration on off-highway machines, chafing against brackets.
  • AdBlue/SCR system: reagent quality and sensors (codes such as 3031.2, 4334.2, 516201.11).
  • Fuel: water and dirt cause misfires and injector faults.
  • Oil: level, viscosity, pressure sensor (code 100.0).
  • Supply to the EMR unit: battery, ground and terminals can produce several codes at once.
  • The control unit itself, last.

Can you keep working?

Entries for critical oil pressure or oil temperature (100.0, 175.0) mean stop the engine. Sensor-circuit codes (FMI 3, 4, 5, 6) do not endanger the engine directly, but protection based on that parameter no longer works, so watch the gauges. If the engine hesitates, loses power or a red lamp comes on, stop.

Questions and answers

What does a number above 516000 mean?

It is a Deutz-specific number, not in J1939. Read the description on this page and confirm it in the manual for your engine.

Code 100.0 and the engine runs smoothly. Can I carry on?

No. The page lists it as critical oil pressure. Stop, check the oil level and measure the pressure with a gauge.

AdBlue sensor reports implausible data. Replace it?

First check the reagent quality, the tank and the connector; replace the sensor only if those are fine.

Mercedes-Benz codes: what to know

Mercedes-Benz records codes in two ways. The free tables cover 118 codes under J1939, where the SPN and the FMI come as a pair. Most records are dealer records, 4059 codes: codes of 4 to 7 characters, such as 0004 or MR 9961, and the description names the model (Atego, Actros, Axor) and the component. The most common areas are sensors and electrical circuits, valves and brakes, pressure and the CAN bus. First establish which format your code is in: an SPN with its FMI is in the free table, and anything else is in the dealer list, where you match the model in the description.

How to read a code

A code has two halves. SPN is what is actually at fault — the sensor, component, or parameter. FMI is what is wrong with it — value out of range, open circuit, short circuit, invalid data. So “SPN 100” alone is not a diagnosis yet, but “100/1” already tells you oil pressure has dropped below normal.

If there are several codes at once

That is almost always how it goes: one fault drags five or six codes behind it. An empty AdBlue tank first throws a level error, then interrupted dosing, then a NOx excess and a torque derate.

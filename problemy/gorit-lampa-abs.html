ABS light is on

Updated: 2026-11-14

The wheel speed sensor is the usual culprit — excess air gap, dirt, a damaged tone ring. The service brakes still work, but ABS is disabled — on a slippery surface you will need to brake more carefully. The code names the axle and side directly.

How to approach it

The ABS lamp means anti-lock braking is not working, but the normal brakes should still work. First check whether a brake or pressure lamp is lit with it, and record all the brake-system codes. If a red brake lamp is also on, do not drive until the pressure is understood. The cause is most often a wheel sensor or the ABS unit power, so check the wheel connectors first.

The reference does not mark any code in this group as a reason to stop.

These codes usually come with it: SPN 789, SPN 790, SPN 791, SPN 792, SPN 793.

What happens if you keep driving

Time you have: Minutes

Part of the brake circuit or ABS/EBS is out of service. The pedal may still feel normal — until the first emergency stop or descent.

Next comes longer stopping distance, a skid on a slick surface, failure on a long downgrade. This is the case where "I will take it slow" does not work.

What to check on the spot

Let the air system build to working pressure and listen for leaks. Don’t drive with faulty brakes.

Can I keep driving

The answer depends on the code, not the light. A yellow light is a warning; a red one means do not move. But even under a yellow light there are faults that require stopping right away — low oil pressure, overheating, lost brake pressure. Enter the code — the reference will tell you plainly whether to keep driving or stop.

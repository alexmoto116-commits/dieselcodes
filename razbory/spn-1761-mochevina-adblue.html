SPN 1761: DEF tank level - how far can you go

Updated: 2026-11-16

In short. The engine will not stall in the middle of the road, but the unit applies protection in steps: a lamp, then a power drop, then a speed limit. Top up the DEF at the first opportunity.

What the code shows

SPN 1761 in J1939 is Aftertreatment 1 Diesel Exhaust Fluid Tank Level: the level of the exhaust treatment fluid in the tank.

FMIMeaningWhat to do
1Level below normal, most severeTop up DEF
17Below normal, low urgency (Cummins: FC 3497)Top up DEF
18Below normal, medium urgency (Cummins: FC 3498)Top up urgently
3Sensor circuit voltage above normal or short to positiveCheck connector and wiring
4Sensor circuit voltage below normal or short to groundCheck connector and wiring

FMI 10, 11 and 13 are rarer: level changing too fast, cause not determined, sensor out of calibration. FMI 1, 17 and 18 are about the level itself, FMI 3 and 4 about the sensor electrics.

How far can you go

There is no universal "kilometres to the limit": the number of steps and when they engage depend on the manufacturer, model year and the code itself. Usually a lamp comes on first, then power drops, then speed may be limited. Exact thresholds are in your vehicle manual. So top up the DEF at the first opportunity and do not wait for the limits.

What the driver does

  1. Top up DEF of the proper quality (not water) in the tank with the blue cap.
  2. Check whether the level on the dashboard rose and the code cleared. Sometimes the unit needs time or an ignition restart, and sometimes a workshop clears it with a scanner.
  3. For FMI 3 or 4, unplug the level sensor connector and inspect it: oxidation and DEF crystals occur. Check the wires.
  4. Tank full but the code remains: the sensor or circuit is at fault, see a workshop.

When to see a workshop

  • DEF topped up but the dashboard level does not change or the code returns: level sensor and circuit diagnostics;
  • FMI 3 or 4 repeats after cleaning the connector: check the wiring and the unit;
  • do not disable the system or fit emulators: that does not remove the problem and codes keep piling up.

Questions and answers

Can I drive with SPN 1761?

Yes, but not far: the unit applies protection in steps - a lamp, then a power drop, then a speed limit. Top up the DEF at the first opportunity.

How many kilometres until the limit?

There is no single number; it depends on the manufacturer, model year and code. See your vehicle manual.

What do FMI 3 and 4 mean for SPN 1761?

The level sensor's electrical circuit: voltage above normal or short to positive (3), below normal or to ground (4). There may be enough DEF.

The code stayed after topping up. What now?

Wait or restart the ignition; sometimes a workshop clears it with a scanner. If it returns, the level sensor and its circuit need diagnostics.
